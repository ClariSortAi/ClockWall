WHY THESE ARE VENDORED. Every part of this watch is metallic, and a metal has
no diffuse component - it can only show you what it reflects. The environment
is therefore not lighting, it IS the material, and the hand-built four-node sky
that used to stand in for it was the direct cause of the plate rendering black,
the steel rendering white, the bezel rendering dark and every chamfer facet
coming back unlit. It also concealed an outright bug for several passes: it fed
a SPHERICAL gradient from a normalized direction vector, so it evaluated to a
single flat grey and produced no sky at all.

A measured studio environment has real softboxes in real positions with real
falloff. Polished metal put in front of one looks right immediately and needs
no tuning, which is the whole point of using one.
